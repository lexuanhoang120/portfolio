Research Focus

My current research focuses on computer-use agents and GUI grounding. I study methods that improve grounding accuracy at inference time without additional training, using attention-map spatial signals to guide stopping decisions and adaptive cropping.

Confidence-Guided Adaptive Refinement for GUI Grounding

Proposed a training-free refinement method that uses attention-derived confidence to decide when to stop refinement and how much visual context to crop for the next prediction.
